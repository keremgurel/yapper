### Why an LLM picks these pages, and how ours compare

- Entity confusion: GSC homepage queries are entity questions ("is yapper safe", "how do yapper's subscription plans and credits work?", "can yapper generate videos from text prompts?"). Without an About page or llms.txt, engines had no canonical facts and mixed us with other Yappers.
- Roundups with tables and prices match the "reviews features pricing" fan-out modifiers.
- Topics stay separate from SpeakingPractice.ai: Yapper covers creator studio, scripts, teleprompter and talking-head video; SpeakingPractice covers speaking practice.

## Fan-out queries

A fan-out query is the web search an AI engine runs before it answers. Only ChatGPT exposes them (DataForSEO's ChatGPT scraper `fan_out_queries`); Perplexity's field is always empty, and Google AI Overview/AI Mode do not expose theirs. We also ran 41 prompts through `ai_optimization/chat_gpt/llm_responses` (gpt-5.4-mini with web search on): it searched for only 1 of 41, answering the rest from memory and brand entities. That is a finding in itself: for generic prompts, being a known entity (llms.txt, About page, consistent facts across the web) matters as much as ranking.

- ChatGPT searched for 11 of 30 prompts (11 queries). Modifiers: 2026 (9), best (7), pricing (3), app (3), alternative (2).
- Observed: "best teleprompter apps iOS Android 2026 reviews features pricing", "best alternatives to Captions …".
- We rank on 0 fan-out queries in Google. The teleprompter post targets the first; a Captions-alternatives page is the next candidate.
