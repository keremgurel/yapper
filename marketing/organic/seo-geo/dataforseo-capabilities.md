# DataForSEO capabilities

The full capability map, official prices, cadence proposal and monthly cost estimate live in the CELPIP repo:

[keremgurel/celpip-practice — marketing/organic/seo-geo/dataforseo-capabilities.md](https://github.com/keremgurel/celpip-practice/blob/main/marketing/organic/seo-geo/dataforseo-capabilities.md)

This repo stores this site’s baselines only:

- [`rank-tracking/`](rank-tracking/) — weekly Google ranks, SERP features and AI Overview presence
- [`keyword-gaps/`](keyword-gaps/) — Labs ranked keywords, competitor gaps and traffic estimates
- [`link-profile/`](link-profile/) — our DataForSEO backlink index (separate from any outreach folder named `backlinks/`)
- [`technical/`](technical/) — Lighthouse and Instant Pages
- [`keyword-intel/`](keyword-intel/) — search intent, clickstream volume, AI keyword volume, Trends and (for Yapper) YouTube SERPs

Scripts live on the SEO box at `/workspace/seo-experiments/`. Search Console remains the source of truth for whether a change worked. Routines have not been changed yet; the proposed weekly and monthly jobs and the ~$11–$13 all-in monthly DataForSEO cost are in the CELPIP document.
