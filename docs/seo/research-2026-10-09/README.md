## Intent checks and product coverage

Qualitative web searches are not controlled US ranking positions. Competitor pages establish the kind of offer a visitor sees, not proof of those vendors' performance claims.

- **Ideas / Brain / Lab:** [Hootsuite's generator](https://www.hootsuite.com/social-media-tools/content-ideas-generator) and [Contentideas.io](https://contentideas.io/) offer free generators. Studio's distinct reason to buy is retained personal context and an idea-to-video workflow. Implemented a contextual generator title and a substantive “how to generate content ideas” worksheet, with an explicit no-AI path. Do not promise free unlimited generation or automatic duplicate detection.
- **Scripts / hooks:** Keep the existing video-specific target and editable outlines. Generic script queries mix screenplays and synthetic script-to-video tools. Future guide: turn one real idea into a 45-second talking-head outline, with examples and editorial checks. No fabricated “viral” guarantee.
- **Teleprompter / recording:** [Recito](https://recito.suprun.ca/mac-teleprompter) and [CursorClip](https://cursorclip.com/features/teleprompter/) show Mac recording use cases. Updated existing title, description and natural-reading guidance. Studio's advantage is script-to-take-to-edit continuity. Do not claim voice tracking, invisible screen sharing or Windows support from competitors' features.
- **One-click / transcript editing:** [TextCut](https://textcut.app/) and [BCut](https://www.bcut.app/) show demand for words, pauses and retake cleanup. Keep one canonical editing page for this cluster. Future detailed walkthrough should show a real before/after and the review step; no invented under-one-minute benchmark or “perfect” cut claim.
- **Captions / dictionary:** Maintain `/features/automatic-captions`; next research should separate burned-in video captions, subtitle-file workflows and social-post copy. Only advertise supported formats. A names-and-specialist-vocabulary example would demonstrate the personal dictionary better than a generic AI claim.
- **Poster / cross-posting:** [Metricool's scheduling tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T7G1A5YpjII) and [WeRepost](https://werepost.ai/) show vertical-video distribution intent. Target task-specific Reels/Shorts/TikTok scheduling language where availability permits. Do not advertise Reddit, unsupported channels or automatic reposting of every upload.
- **Calendar:** [Hopper's planner](https://www.hopperhq.com/tools/content-planner/) attracts free calendar-generation intent. Studio's page should sell production status connected to the actual video. Keep planning dates distinct from scheduled delivery.
- **Library:** New compact homepage stack links to the project library and calendar. Avoid generic DAM/storage enterprise queries; demonstrate idea, script and video together. Library is a product differentiator even without large measured query demand.
