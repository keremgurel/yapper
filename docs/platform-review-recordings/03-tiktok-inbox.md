# Recording 03 — TikTok inbox and finishing on the phone

File: `03-tiktok-inbox-and-finish.mp4`. About 2–3 minutes plus processing. Covers `video.upload`.

## Before filming

Use the same authorized sandbox account in Yapper and the TikTok phone app. Prepare a second original take. Enable TikTok notifications and start the phone screen recording before the upload. Verify inbox delivery works during rehearsal; do not repeatedly send uploads while an attempt is pending.

## 1. Explain the separate choice

**Do:** Show **Connections → TikTok → Connect**, the real permission grant, and return to the intended account.

**Say:**

> “Hi TikTok review team. This recording shows Yapper's optional inbox workflow. The creator chooses this when they want to finish the video inside TikTok. I connect the same TikTok account that is signed in on my phone.”

## 2. Choose the inbox method

**Do:** Open **Poster → Made in Yapper**, select the second take, choose only TikTok, and click **Publish to 1 destination**. Set **Posting method → Send to TikTok inbox to finish there**. Play the preview and show the explanation.

**Say:**

> “I choose my original video and select Send to TikTok inbox to finish there. Yapper explains that I will open the TikTok notification, add the caption, and finish posting in TikTok. Sending to the inbox does not mean the video has been published.”

## 3. Send and confirm delivery

**Do:** Click **Publish 1 video to 1 platform** once. Show processing and wait for confirmed inbox delivery. Keep the sheet open.

**Say:**

> “I authorize the transfer. Yapper now waits for TikTok to confirm delivery to the inbox.”

**Say only after delivery is confirmed:**

> “TikTok has confirmed inbox delivery. I will now open the delivered content on my phone.”

## 4. Open the notification and finish

**Do:** Switch to the phone recording. Open the actual **Your content from Yapper is ready** notification, from its banner or TikTok Inbox. Show the imported video. Enter `A quick sound check before recording.` as its caption. Review the phone's posting settings; choose **Only you** or equivalent private visibility where offered. Tap TikTok's final **Post** button and show the completed video.

**Say:**

> “Here is the notification from TikTok for the content sent by Yapper. Opening it takes me to the imported video. I add my caption and review the posting settings here. I choose private visibility for this test and tap Post in TikTok. The video is now on my account.”

Use the actual button wording if it differs. If the notification is missing, do not substitute an ordinary upload from the phone's photo library.

## 5. Disconnect

**Do:** Return to desktop **Connections → TikTok → Disconnect** and show the disconnected row.

**Say:**

> “The creator can disconnect TikTok here. This demonstrates confirmed inbox delivery followed by the creator completing the post in TikTok.”

## Finish check

Show both halves: confirmed delivery in Yapper, then the actual TikTok notification opening that transferred video. Keep the matching phone account visible. End with the completed post, not just an upload bar or generic TikTok draft.
