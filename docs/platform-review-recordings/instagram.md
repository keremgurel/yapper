# Instagram — one complete recording

Aim for about 2–3 minutes plus necessary processing. Start with the shared short introduction. Use an authorized Creator or Business account and an existing Reel with working insights. Rehearse first: a fallback zero view count does not prove insights access.

## 1. Connect

**Do:** Connections → Instagram → Connect. Show Instagram's real permissions, authorize, and return to the connected handle.

> “I connect my Instagram Professional account and review the requested permissions. Yapper then shows which account is connected.”

## 2. Show existing media and insights

**Do:** Poster → Instagram source tab. Show a real existing Reel's title, thumbnail, and available view count. Keep the number readable.

> “I can review my existing Instagram videos and their view counts here. This uses the account's media and insights permissions.”

## 3. Review an original video

**Do:** Made in Yapper → select your camera-check clip, or Add video for its export. Play the preview. Select only Instagram and edit its caption. Click Publish to 1 destination and show the correct account in the final sheet.

> “I select an original video, preview it, and edit the caption. I choose my connected Instagram account as the destination.”

## 4. Publish and show the actual Reel

**Do:** Click Publish 1 video to 1 platform. Show processing and confirmation, then open the actual Reel in Instagram. Show the profile, video, and caption. Label any processing time cut.

> “I authorize the publication. Here is the completed Reel on the same Instagram account, with the caption I reviewed.”

Say the result sentence only after the actual Reel appears.

## 5. Close

**Do:** Connections → Instagram → Disconnect. Show Connect again, then briefly show the deletion/contact section at https://ypr.app/privacy.

> “I can disconnect Instagram here. Our privacy policy explains data handling and deletion requests.”

This one recording covers basic account/media access, insights, and content publishing. Follow Meta's exact permission prompts when attaching it; use excerpts only if the review form requires them.
