# TikTok — one complete recording

Aim for about 3–4 minutes, allowing more time when consent or processing needs it. Use the short shared introduction in the recording index. Keep the settings and actual results readable. Export one MP4/MOV under the current 50 MB limit.

Before filming: verify sandbox credentials and authorized accounts. Have your original sample clip and a second take ready, plus TikTok on your phone. Direct Post requires a private test account and Only me. To show the public-video library, use an authorized account with an existing public video first; explicitly show the switch to a private test account or the same account's privacy change before Direct Post.

## 1. Connect and show account data

**Do:** Connections → TikTok → Connect. Show the real permissions, authorize, and return. Show the connected name, statistics, and View profile link. Then Poster → TikTok: show existing public videos.

> “I connect my TikTok account and review the permissions. Back in Yapper, I can see my connected profile, account statistics, and existing public videos.”

## 2. Move to the private test account

**Do:** Show the account's private setting in TikTok before publishing. If switching accounts, disconnect and connect the authorized private account on camera. Keep the transition clear.

> “For Direct Post, I am using a private sandbox account with Only me visibility while our public-posting review is pending.”

## 3. Review the post

**Do:** Poster → Made in Yapper → select the original clip → select only TikTok → edit the caption → Publish to 1 destination. Keep Posting method set to Post to TikTok. Play the preview and show Posting as. Show Choose an audience, then select Only me. Point to the unchecked comments, Duet, and Stitch controls; demonstrate an available control briefly, then restore the intended setting.

> “I review the original video, caption, and destination account. I choose the audience myself. Interactions start off, and settings disabled by TikTok stay unavailable.”

**Do:** Turn Disclose commercial content on. Show the requirement to choose a disclosure, then select Your brand to display its label notice. Show Branded content disabled for private visibility. Turn disclosure off again for the noncommercial camera-tip sample. Leave AI labeling off for your real camera footage.

> “Commercial disclosure and AI labeling are available when applicable. This sample is an original, noncommercial camera recording.”

## 4. Consent, publish, and result

**Do:** Show and check the posting declaration. Click Publish 1 video to 1 platform. Show processing, then confirmed completion. Open the actual video in TikTok and show Only me. Label any processing time cut.

> “I review the declaration and authorize the post. Once processing finishes, I can see the completed video in TikTok with the visibility I selected.”

Only say the second sentence after the actual result appears.

## 5. Inbox alternative in the same recording

**Do:** Select the second original take in Poster. Select only TikTok, open the final sheet, and choose Send to TikTok inbox to finish there. Send once and wait for confirmed delivery. Switch to your phone recording, open Your content from Yapper is ready, add the caption, review the phone's privacy settings, and finish posting. Show the resulting video.

> “The creator can also choose to finish inside TikTok. I select the inbox option. After delivery is confirmed, I open TikTok's notification, review the video and settings, and complete the post here.”

## 6. Close

**Do:** Connections → TikTok → Disconnect; show Connect again.

> “The creator can disconnect TikTok at any time from Connections.”

Do not substitute an ordinary phone upload for the inbox notification. If a result remains pending, preserve the footage and resolve it before recording the final demonstration.
