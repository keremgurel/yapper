# Recording 04 — Instagram connection, insights, and Reel

File: `04-instagram-connect-insights-publish.mp4`. About 3–4 minutes plus processing. Covers `instagram_business_basic`, `instagram_business_manage_insights`, and `instagram_business_content_publish`.

## Before filming

Use an authorized Instagram **Creator or Business** account. Yapper uses Instagram Login directly; this Instagram flow does not require the creator to connect a Facebook Page. Have an existing Reel with available, preferably nonzero, views. Confirm that those views genuinely load before recording: the current library can display zero when insights are unavailable, so zero alone does not prove successful insights access.

## 1. Introduce

**Do:** Show Yapper's website and app. Optionally insert 15–20 seconds of the creation walkthrough.

**Say:**

> “Hi Meta review team. I'm Kerem, the founder of Yapper. Yapper helps creators develop, record, edit, and publish original videos. This recording shows an Instagram Professional account connecting to Yapper, reviewing its own videos and insights, and publishing an original Reel.”

## 2. Authorize Instagram

**Do:** Open **Connections → Instagram → Connect**. Show Instagram's account and requested permissions, authorize, and return to Yapper. Hold on **Connected** and the matching handle.

**Say:**

> “I choose Connect for Instagram. I am authorizing my Professional account through Instagram's permission screen. After authorization, Yapper displays the connected account so I can confirm the publishing destination.”

## 3. Show media and insights

**Do:** Open **Poster → Instagram** in the Video source tabs. Wait for real media. Point to an existing Reel's title, thumbnail, and view count beside the eye icon. Open the same Reel in Instagram separately to establish that it belongs to the connected account.

**Say:**

> “This source tab displays the connected account's Instagram media. The basic permission lets Yapper identify the account and read its media. The insights permission supplies the view counts shown alongside those videos, helping the creator review their own content.”

## 4. Review the new Reel

**Do:** Return to **Poster → Made in Yapper**. Choose the original camera-check clip, or use **Add video** for the export. Play its preview. Select only **Instagram** and edit its caption to the camera-check text. Click **Publish to 1 destination** and show the correct Instagram account selected in the final sheet.

**Say:**

> “I now select an original video I recorded, review the preview, and edit its caption. I choose only my connected Instagram account for this post.”

## 5. Publish and prove the result

**Do:** Click **Publish 1 video to 1 platform**. Show processing and confirmation. Open the actual new Reel in Instagram, show the matching profile, play the video, and display its caption.

**Say before sending:**

> “I click Publish to authorize this Reel. Yapper sends the video and caption through Instagram's content-publishing integration.”

**Say only after the Reel is present:**

> “The publication is complete. This is the same video and caption on the connected Instagram account.”

## 6. Disconnect and privacy instructions

**Do:** Open **Connections → Instagram → Disconnect** and show **Connect** again. Open `https://ypr.app/privacy` and scroll to its data-deletion/contact instructions.

**Say:**

> “The creator can disconnect Instagram from Connections. Yapper's privacy policy also explains data handling and how to request deletion.”

## Finish check

Keep login, matching identity, actual media, genuine insights, editable post, deliberate publish, and the resulting Reel. If Meta requests individual permission videos, use the full recording where accepted or export labeled excerpts retaining login and the relevant feature. Do not submit an insights explanation without demonstrating that feature successfully. Follow the actual permission prompts in Meta App Review.
