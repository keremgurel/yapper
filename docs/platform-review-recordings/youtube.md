# YouTube — one complete recording

Aim for about 2–3 minutes plus necessary processing. Start with the shared short introduction. Use a channel you control with existing videos and the integration for project yapper-502200. Branding is verified; scope verification is pending.

Poster currently requests public visibility automatically. There is no YouTube privacy or made-for-kids selector in this UI. Use a sample you are comfortable making public and show the actual visibility in YouTube Studio.

## 1. Connect through Google

**Do:** Connections → YouTube → Connect. Show Google's account chooser, any unverified-app warning, and the complete permission consent. Choose your intended account/channel, authorize, and return to Yapper. Keep authorization-page identity readable; keep passwords and callback authorization codes out of the recording.

> “I connect my YouTube channel through Google. I review the permissions to read my YouTube account and upload videos, then authorize Yapper.”

If a warning appears, add:

> “The YouTube permissions are awaiting verification, so this test connection displays Google's warning.”

## 2. Show read access

**Do:** Show the connected channel in Connections, then Poster → YouTube source tab. Show existing videos and their titles, thumbnails, and view counts. Briefly open an existing user-selected YouTube creator feed in Inspiration, since that use is included in the prepared scope explanation.

> “Yapper shows my connected channel and its video library. It also reads public video information for creator feeds I choose to follow.”

## 3. Review the upload

**Do:** Poster → Made in Yapper → select and preview your original clip → choose only YouTube. Edit the title and description. Choose a video frame for the cover if required. Click Publish to 1 destination, and show the channel and public-visibility notice.

> “I choose my original video, edit its title and description, and confirm the connected channel. This version requests public visibility.”

## 4. Publish and verify in Studio

**Do:** Click Publish 1 video to 1 platform. Show the upload result, then YouTube Studio → Content → the actual new video. Show its title, description, playback, and Visibility. Label any processing time cut.

> “I authorize the upload. Here is that video in YouTube Studio, with the same title and description. This is the actual visibility assigned by YouTube.”

Only narrate the result once the video appears. Read the visible value; do not call it public if Studio says Private. OAuth verification and YouTube's public-upload compliance audit are separate.

## 5. Close

**Do:** Connections → YouTube → Disconnect; show Connect again. Briefly show the deletion/contact instructions at https://ypr.app/privacy.

> “I can disconnect YouTube here. Our privacy policy explains data handling and deletion requests.”

Upload this review recording itself as an Unlisted YouTube video and test its link signed out. Use that link in Google's Data Access form.
