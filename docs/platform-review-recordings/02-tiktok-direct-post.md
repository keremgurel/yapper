# Recording 02 — TikTok Direct Post

File: `02-tiktok-direct-post.mp4`. About 3–5 minutes plus processing. Covers `video.publish` and the actual post-review controls.

## Before filming

Confirm sandbox credentials, authorized test account, and verified media host. Use a **private TikTok test account**. If using recording 01's account, change account privacy before this recording. Keep that account open in TikTok for the result. Use the original noncommercial camera-check clip from recording 00.

Unaudited Direct Post requires Only me and private test accounts. TikTok's requirements include creator identity, editable post information, deliberate settings, disclosures, preview, and consent. The steps below show Yapper's implemented controls. [TikTok Content Sharing Guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/content-sharing-guidelines)

## 1. Introduce and reconnect

**Do:** Open **Connections → TikTok → Connect**, show permissions, authorize, and return. Briefly show the test account's private setting in TikTok, then return to Yapper.

**Say:**

> “Hi TikTok review team. This recording demonstrates Yapper's Direct Post workflow. I am using an authorized private sandbox account. Public Direct Post approval is still pending, so this demonstration will publish with Only me visibility. I am connecting this account and granting the posting permission.”

## 2. Choose the original video

**Do:** Open **Poster → Made in Yapper** and select your clip, or use **Add video** for its export. Select only **TikTok**. Review the destination caption and click **Publish to 1 destination** to open the final sheet; this is not the final send.

**Say:**

> “This is the original video I recorded. I select TikTok as the destination and review the caption before opening the final posting controls.”

## 3. Preview and identity

**Do:** Keep **Posting method → Post to TikTok**. Play the preview, show **Posting as** with the correct name/handle, and edit **Caption** to the prepared camera-check copy.

**Say:**

> “The review screen shows the video and the TikTok account that will receive it. I can play the preview and edit the caption. Yapper loads the current posting options for this creator when this screen opens.”

## 4. Visibility and interactions

**Do:** Show **Who can see this video? → Choose an audience** before making a selection. Choose **Only me**. Point to **Allow comments**, **Allow Duet**, and **Allow Stitch**, initially unchecked. If an interaction is available, check and uncheck it to demonstrate control. Leave them off for the sample; do not try to enable disabled options.

**Say:**

> “No audience has been selected for me. I choose Only me for this test. Comments, Duet, and Stitch start turned off. The creator chooses which available interactions to enable, and options disabled by the TikTok account remain unavailable.”

## 5. Disclosures

**Do:** Check **Disclose commercial content**. Leave its sub-options unselected briefly to show the explanatory message. Select **Your brand** and show the **Promotional content** notice. Point to **Branded content**, disabled because Only me is selected. Turn **Disclose commercial content** back off for this noncommercial sample. Leave **Label this video as AI-generated** off for the real camera recording.

**Say:**

> “If a video promotes a business or another brand, the creator can disclose that here. Selecting Your brand shows the promotional-content notice. Branded content cannot use private visibility. I am only demonstrating the controls; this camera-tip video does not promote a brand, so I turn disclosure off before sending it. This footage uses my real camera and voice, so I also leave the AI-generated label off.”

## 6. Consent and final send

**Do:** Scroll to the posting declaration and show its Music Usage Confirmation link. Review and check the declaration. Click **Publish 1 video to 1 platform** once.

**Say:**

> “I have reviewed the video and its settings. I read the posting declaration and authorize this post. I now click Publish to send this one video to the selected TikTok account.”

## 7. Processing and actual private post

**Do:** Show processing and wait for confirmation. If needed, label a time cut and use **Check publish status** in the same sheet. Open the exact post on the connected TikTok account, play it, and show Only me. Do not rely on the public-video library to display a private post.

**Say while pending:**

> “TikTok is processing the video. Yapper checks the posting status while this completes.”

**Say only after confirmed success and opening the actual post:**

> “The post is now confirmed. Here is the same video on the connected TikTok account, with Only me visibility. This completed through Direct Post without a separate manual publishing step in TikTok.”

## 8. Disconnect

**Do:** Return to **Connections → TikTok → Disconnect** and show **Connect**.

**Say:**

> “The creator can disconnect their account here. That completes the Direct Post demonstration.”

## Finish check

Retain the identity, blank initial audience, chosen Only me, interaction defaults, disclosures, consent, final click, confirmed result, and actual private post. Do not narrate success if the attempt fails or remains pending. Preserve that footage, fix the flow, and rerecord.
