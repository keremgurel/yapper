# Before recording

Use this once before the four platform scripts. Quoted passages are the full words to say. Camera inset is optional; clear screen footage and narration are enough. Keep the inset away from account names, permissions, settings, and results. Suggested lengths are editorial guidance, not platform requirements.

## Prepare one original sample video

Record this 15–20 second script vertically, at 1080 × 1920 and 30 fps, using your real camera and voice:

> “Before I start a recording, I check three things. I put the camera at eye level, face a window for soft light, and record a few seconds to check the sound. Those simple checks help me make a clearer video.”

Save the export as `camera-check-demo.mp4`. Use plain captions if desired. No music, promotional overlay, or Yapper/platform watermark is needed. This is a practical camera tip, not a sponsored advertisement or AI-generated likeness. Reuse the source across platforms; record a second take for the TikTok inbox example so the two TikTok results are distinguishable.

Use these post fields:

- Title where needed: `Three checks before recording`
- Caption/description: `Camera at eye level. Face the light. Check the sound. Three simple checks before recording.`
- Second-take caption: `A quick sound check before recording.`

## Set up the screen

Prepare Editor and Poster **before** starting the recording. In the native Editor, use **Export & Post** once and wait until the finished edit opens in Poster. This renders and uploads the edit to your saved Poster library; it does not publish to social accounts. Leave that saved video ready in Poster. During the demo, briefly show the editable project, then click Poster and select its already-prepared export. Say “Here is the finished video, which I've already prepared for posting.” You do not need to perform or record the export again. If you change the edit afterward, prepare a new export so the two views match. An existing final MP4 can also be added through Poster's **Add video** without rendering it again.

1. Enable Do Not Disturb and close unrelated private tabs. Allow TikTok notifications on the phone for the inbox section of the TikTok recording.
2. Sign into Yapper and the platform off camera. Leave that platform disconnected in Yapper so the actual permission grant appears in the recording. Do not remove permissions from unrelated apps.
3. Record at 1080p with readable text and clear microphone audio. Show the browser address bar at the beginning of the web flow. For the Mac app, show it opening and transitioning into any browser authorization window.
4. Use the native Mac app for the Editor portion: the web Editor currently hands off to the native editor. The hosted integration pages are `https://ypr.app/studio/connections` and `https://ypr.app/studio/poster`.
5. Keep account selection, permission review, authorization, return to Yapper, final Publish click, and destination result visible. Pause for passwords/two-factor codes; resume before consent. Do not show callback authorization codes. Label any processing time cut.
6. Select only the destination being demonstrated. The final sheet should say **Publish 1 video to 1 platform**.
7. Rehearse the flow once. If a post stays pending, keep the sheet open and use **Check publish status**. Do not repeatedly start new uploads. Preserve errors for diagnosis and record the final demonstration once it works.

## Export and submit

Keep the raw recordings. Export the single TikTok recording as MP4/MOV under 50 MB. A readable 720p export can help; split footage instead of making text illegible. First-time TikTok review requires the sandbox and demonstrations of all selected scopes. [TikTok review guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/our-guidelines-app-review-guidelines)

For Meta, follow the actual permission prompts in App Review. Export focused excerpts if it requests individual permission videos, retaining the login and relevant working feature. Its public documentation could not be fetched reliably during this drafting pass; the current form remains the authority for upload formatting.

Upload the Google/YouTube review recording to YouTube as **Unlisted**, test the link signed out, and use that link in Google's Data Access form. The console asks for coverage of every OAuth client assigned to project `yapper-502200`. [Google verification guidance](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13461325?hl=en)

Control names and behavior in this pack were checked against Yapper's current source. Provider authorization wording can vary by language, account, and existing grants; follow the real screen when Continue/Allow labels differ.
