# Before recording

Use this once before the active platform scripts. Quoted passages are the full words to say. Camera inset is optional; clear screen footage and narration are enough. Keep the inset away from account names, permissions, settings, and results. Suggested lengths are editorial guidance, not platform requirements.

## Prepare one original sample video

Record this 15–20 second script vertically, at 1080 × 1920 and 30 fps, using your real camera and voice:

> “Before I start a recording, I check three things. I put the camera at eye level, face a window for soft light, and record a few seconds to check the sound. Those simple checks help me make a clearer video.”

Save the export as `camera-check-demo.mp4`. Use plain captions if desired. No music, promotional overlay, or Yapper/platform watermark is needed. This is a practical camera tip, not a sponsored advertisement or AI-generated likeness. Reuse this same source clip for Instagram and YouTube.

Use these post fields:

- Title where needed: `Three checks before recording`
- Caption/description: `Camera at eye level. Face the light. Check the sound. Three simple checks before recording.`

## Set up the screen

For the current Instagram and Google reviews, use the **web app only**. There is no need to record Lab, Recorder, the native Editor, AI generation, Facebook, TikTok, or scheduling. Upload the finished MP4 in Poster with **Uploads → Add video → choose the MP4 file**. The reviewer instructions already use this route. TikTok is approved; do not record another TikTok demo unless TikTok requests one.

1. Enable Do Not Disturb and close unrelated private tabs. Use English for provider consent screens and narration.
2. Prepare a YouTube channel with existing videos and an Instagram Creator or Business account with an existing Reel whose insights work. While Meta is in development, use an eligible account added to the app's roles/test setup. Ordinary customer access needs approved Advanced Access.
3. Rehearse connection, media loading and posting before filming. A saved connection or Meta's “API calls completed” indicator does not prove the whole flow works. In Poster, a dash means Instagram insights are unavailable; use a Reel with a real numeric view count. A genuine zero is supported, but an older Reel with known views is clearer evidence.
4. Record at 1080p with readable text and microphone audio. A camera inset is optional. Show Yapper's address and app name. Use `https://studio.ypr.app/studio/connections` and `https://studio.ypr.app/studio/poster`.
5. Leave the demonstrated platform disconnected before recording its real consent flow. For Google's website-login segment, use a separate signed-out Yapper tab. Pause for passwords and two-factor codes; resume before consent. Hide callback authorization codes and never show client secrets.
6. Keep account selection, complete permissions, authorization, return to Yapper, final Publish click, and destination result visible. Label processing time cuts.
7. Select only the destination being demonstrated. The final sheet should say **Publish 1 video to 1 platform**.
8. If a post stays pending, keep the sheet open and use **Check publish status**. Do not repeatedly start uploads. Preserve errors for diagnosis.

Opening narration can be one sentence: “Yapper lets creators review an original video and publish it to accounts they connect.” Then begin the relevant script.

## Export and submit

Keep the raw recordings and the original sample MP4 until both reviews are complete.

The live Meta draft was inspected on October 23: it has three separate screencast uploads, and **each permission asks to include the OAuth flow**. Make three focused exports from one Instagram master recording; see the exact mapping in [Instagram](instagram.md). Follow the upload control’s current format/size limits.

Upload the Google/YouTube review recording to YouTube as **Unlisted**, test the link signed out, and use that link in Google's Data Access form. The console asks for coverage of every OAuth client assigned to project `yapper-502200`. [Google verification guidance](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13461325?hl=en)

Control names and behavior in this pack were checked against Yapper's current source. Provider authorization wording can vary by language, account, and existing grants; follow the real screen when Continue/Allow labels differ.

## Production rehearsal gate

Do these once without recording at https://studio.ypr.app:

1. Connections: connect the intended YouTube channel and Instagram Business/Creator account; approve all permissions and verify each displayed identity. Reload and confirm both remain connected.
2. Poster: open each channel source and refresh it. Check existing thumbnails/titles, and a numeric Instagram view count for an older Reel. A dash or loading/error state must be resolved before recording.
3. Poster → Uploads → Add video: upload the original MP4, open it and play the preview. Select YouTube only, fill title/description, choose Unlisted, answer audience/synthetic-media questions truthfully, and publish once. Open the actual result in YouTube Studio and verify playback, identity and visibility.
4. Use the same source for Instagram only. Review preview/caption/account, publish once, then open the actual Reel and confirm playback and caption. Leave time for platform processing and use Check publish status if offered.
5. Disconnect YouTube in Connections. Confirm its library disappears and the original MP4 remains in Uploads. Reconnect and verify the channel returns. Google may ask to reauthorize website sign-in too.
6. Rehearse the dedicated Meta reviewer Yapper login using the credentials saved in Meta. Do not record or share passwords/OTP codes.

Only after these pass, record the two master walkthroughs in the platform guides. Google needs an Unlisted link to the demo recording; Meta needs three permission-specific exports, each including the real Instagram OAuth flow. Scheduling and Facebook are excluded.
