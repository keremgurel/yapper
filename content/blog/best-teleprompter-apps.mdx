## How to choose a teleprompter app

Ask four questions before comparing features:

1. **Where will the camera be?** A phone app is easiest when you record on the phone. A laptop or browser prompter suits a webcam. A DSLR needs either a tablet in a glass rig or hardware like Elgato Prompter, which uses beam-splitter glass so the text sits over the lens ([what is a teleprompter](/blog/what-is-a-teleprompter) explains how the glass works).
2. **Do you want voice scrolling?** Voice tracking keeps pace when you speed up, slow down or improvise. Fixed-speed scrolling is fine for short scripts once you have tested your pace.
3. **What happens after the take?** If every recording then goes into an editor, a caption tool and a scheduler, the prompter is only a small part of the job.
4. **How long are your scripts?** For a 30-second TikTok you barely need one. For a 10-minute YouTube lesson, a prompter saves the most retakes.

## The apps, one by one

### Teleprompter.com

Its home page describes a "studio-grade teleprompter app with VoiceGlide": the text follows your voice, pauses when you pause and picks up where you left off, with cloud-synced scripts across iOS, Android, Mac and web. The Starter plan is free. Pro is $19.99 a month, or $7.50 a month billed yearly, and adds 4K recording and subtitles. The same company makes CuePrompter.

### PromptSmart

PromptSmart's site leads with its patented VoiceTrack: the script "automatically scrolls as you speak, stops when you pause or improvise". It lists VoiceTrack scrolling in English. It is a long-standing choice for presenters who record on an iPad or iPhone.

### Speakflow

Speakflow calls itself "the teleprompter that follows your voice". You can scroll by voice or manually, sync a script across devices with its remote, and use it in any modern browser or in its apps for Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad. It has a free plan; Plus is $15 a month billed yearly.

### BIGVU

BIGVU combines a teleprompter with captions and AI video tools, and its own guide to teleprompter apps puts its voice-following prompter first. Its pricing page lists a free plan, Starter at $12 a month, AI Pro at $19 a month ($229 a year) and Max at $49 a month.

### CuePrompter

CuePrompter turns your browser into a teleprompter with "no signup, no downloads", an adjustable scroll speed and no watermarks. It is free for commercial and non-commercial use, which makes it the easiest way to try reading from a prompter before paying for anything.

### Elgato Prompter

A hardware prompter: a screen and beam-splitter glass that sit in front of a DSLR or webcam, so you read with natural eye contact. Elgato lists it at $219.99 (down from $299.99). Use it if you record at a desk every day and want the text directly over the lens.

## How to sound natural on a teleprompter

- **Write for the ear.** Short sentences you would actually say. Our guide to [writing a video script](/blog/how-to-write-a-video-script) has templates.
- **Use talking points for loose videos.** A full script suits tutorials; bullet points suit opinion and story videos.
- **Set the text close to the lens.** The farther the words are from the camera, the more viewers notice your eyes moving.
- **Do a 20-second test take** at your planned speed before the real one.
- **Leave pauses in.** It is easier to cut a pause than to fix a rushed line. Then see [how to make a talking head video](/blog/talking-head-video) for lighting and framing.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free teleprompter app?

CuePrompter is free for any use and runs in a browser with no signup. Teleprompter.com, Speakflow and BIGVU also have free plans with limits.

### Which teleprompter apps follow your voice?

Teleprompter.com (VoiceGlide), PromptSmart (VoiceTrack) and Speakflow all say on their sites that the script scrolls as you speak and stops when you pause. BIGVU's own guide describes its prompter as voice-following.

### Is there a teleprompter app for Windows?

Speakflow lists a Windows app as well as browser use. Browser-based prompters such as CuePrompter work on any Windows computer.

### Do I need a teleprompter for TikTok or Reels?

Not always. For clips under 30 seconds, talking points on a card often sound more natural. A teleprompter helps most for longer or information-heavy videos. See our [TikTok script guide](/blog/tiktok-script).
